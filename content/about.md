## Me Me Me {#me-me-me}

I'm Shreyas (_sh(ray)-us_) Ragavan.

My passions typically revolve around flexing my creative and analytical muscles in finding multi-disciplinary solutions to problems. I enjoy trying to figure out how things work. I'm mostly a dreamer and a [hacker](http://catb.org/~esr/faqs/hacker-howto.html).

I started my career as a mechanical engineer and computational physicist. My favorite subjects were Combustion, Aerodynamics and particularly Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD); and applying these subjects in the areas of design engineering.

My current areas of fascination lie in the applications of Data Science and Engineering as well as Machine Learning. CFD, being all about modeling physics incidentally has a number of aspects that are closely aligned with many aspects important to Data Science ([or whatever that is](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10618600.2017.1384734)), and seemed a natural choice to consider.

Along the way, I've worked in R&amp;D, Manufacturing, Operations Research, Sales and in Software Engineering. A few years ago, I took a leap of faith, from Mumbai, India and landed on the East Coast, in Toronto (Canada) and then made my way over to the west coast, near Vancouver, British Columbia.

My hobbies tend to lie in the directions of reading, writing and drawing, and of course - the eternal quest to become a better programmer and building something _cool_, if not useful. Nowadays, I'm also trying to 'live more', by experimenting with 'interesting things' that exist outside a computer :)

I love using [Emacs](<https://www.emacswiki.org>)! and am currently one of the many Admins for the [EmacsWiki](<https://www.emacswiki.org>).
