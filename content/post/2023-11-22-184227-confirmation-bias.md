Where pre-conceived notions and beliefs affect the current decision
irrationally. Information is not processed neutrally.

Another example: if the goal post is allowed to move after receiving
the information, then confirmation bias can distort the path to the
correct decision.

One of the best antidotes to ConfirmationBias is to set the decision
criteria in advance of receiving the information, and consciously monitor it.
